# Re-cut plates, 3 September 2026

Three replacement plates issued after Rev J, with apertures from hand measurements. Context and a comparison with Rev J are in [`../REVISIONS.md`](../REVISIONS.md).

Material: 3.0 mm clear cast acrylic. Red = cut through, blue = engrave only, grey notes layer output off.

| File | Stock | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| `1_TEST_TFT_MIC_SPEAKER.dxf` | 230 × 138 mm | Cut this first. A display back-support plate (108 × 96, inner 67 × 53), a test card with one Ø53 speaker hole and its four M3 on a 45 × 45 square and one Ø11 microphone hole, and a front-window test piece with the 63 × 45 window and the 5 × 10 button slot |
| `2_THREE_PLATES.dxf` | 578 × 198 mm | 01 front, 03 left, 04 right. Cut only after the test file passes |

## Checks on the test piece, in order

1. Shield on the window test piece, screen down: the screen sits in the 63 × 45 window and the on-board button lands in the slot beside it.
2. Shield into the back support's recess without forcing; the four screw holes line up with the front piece.
3. Speaker body through the Ø53; all four flange bolts drop into the four holes.
4. Microphone into the Ø11 and it stays put.

## Two things to watch

- The bridge between the button slot and the display window is 2.0 mm wide, thinner than the 3 mm used everywhere else on this design. Do not lever against it and do not sand that edge.
- With Ø53 and bolts on a 45 mm square there is 3.7 mm of acrylic between the speaker hole and each bolt hole. Nylon washer under every nut; finger-tight plus a quarter turn.

03 left and 04 right are mirrors, not duplicates.
